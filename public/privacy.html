Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026 • Version 1.0

Your Privacy & Pastoral Trust

Rhema Inner Court Gospel Church (Word Assembly Worldwide) is committed to protecting your personal information and honoring the pastoral confidentiality of all prayer requests, giving records, and community interactions.

1. Ministry & Platform Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to all digital applications and portals operated by Rhema Inner Court Gospel Church (Word Assembly Worldwide) ("RICGCW", "the Ministry", "we", "us", or "our"), including the RICGCW Connect mobile application (Android & iOS) and the RICGCW Web Portal (https://ricgcw.web.app).

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide church administrative, pastoral, and community features:

  • Member Identity & Profile: Full name, email address, telephone number, residential address, date of birth, home sanctuary branch (Langma, Mallam, Kokrobitey, Diaspora), and ministry department.
  • Service Check-In & Safety Tags: Self check-in timestamps, service attendance, and Child Court safety pickup tag tokens.
  • Prayer Petitions & Pastoral Counseling: Spiritual prayer petitions, thanksgiving testimonies, and notes submitted to pastoral counselors.
  • Kingdom Stewardship & Giving: Member transaction logs, contribution categories (Tithes, Offerings, Welfare, Missions), and amounts. Note: Payment cards and Mobile Money PINs are processed securely by PCI-DSS licensed processors and are never collected or stored by RICGCW.
  • App Diagnostics: Device type, OS version, and crash diagnostics strictly used for app stability and performance.
3. Pastoral Privilege & Spiritual Confidentiality

All personal spiritual data, prayer requests, and counseling submissions are protected under pastoral privilege. Petitions marked as Confidential are exclusively accessible to Overseer Rev. Nicholas Dobeng and the ordained Pastoral Council for spiritual intercession and counseling. We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your prayer requests or member details to third-party commercial entities.

4. Device Permissions & Background Services

The RICGCW Connect mobile app requests the following Android and iOS device permissions:

  • Foreground Service & Media Playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Required for uninterrupted 24/7 background streaming of church radio broadcasts and sermon audio while multitasking or when the screen is locked.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver service reminders, live stream start alerts, daily scriptures, and urgent pastoral announcements.
  • Network State & Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to fetch sermon libraries, live audio feeds, and synchronize member profile records.
5. Child & Family Safety (Children's Court)

Sanctuary safety is paramount. All Child Court check-in records and child pickup security tokens are managed directly and exclusively by verified parents or authorized legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13 without parental consent.

6. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

You have full rights to access, modify, export, or permanently delete your account and associated records:

  • Online Deletion Request Portal: Submit a request via our official Account & Data Deletion Request Page.
  • In-App Deletion: In the mobile app, navigate to Profile > Security > Delete Account.
  • Email Deletion Request: Send an email to privacy@ricgcw.org or info@ricgcw.org with your registered account details. All associated data will be purged within 7 business days.
7. Third-Party Service Integrations

We work with trusted industry providers for infrastructure and security:

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore: Encrypted data storage, user authentication, and crash telemetry.
  • Zeno Media: Content delivery network for 24/7 live audio radio streams.
  • Payment Processors: Licensed banks and mobile money gateways.
